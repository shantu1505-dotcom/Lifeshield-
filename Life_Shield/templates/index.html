What is LifeShield?

A connected wearable system designed to assist with patient monitoring, location awareness and emergency response.

A smarter way to stay connected with patient safety.

LifeShield combines a wearable belt-based device with a software platform to provide continuous monitoring of selected patient information.

The belt is designed to be worn around the patient's waist. Depending on the connected hardware, physiological and movement-related information can be collected and transmitted to the LifeShield application.

When configured emergency conditions are detected, the system can process the event and provide relevant emergency information through the configured alert workflow.

How to Wear the LifeShield Belt

Correct placement is important for reliable operation of the wearable system.

  • Wear the belt around the waist/abdomen.
  • Position the sensing module according to the hardware instructions.
  • Make sure the belt is secure without being unnecessarily tight.
  • Check that the device is powered and connected before use.
  • Verify that the monitoring application receives the device data after connection.
Important: The LifeShield belt is intended to be worn around the waist. It should not be worn around the neck.

What the System Can Monitor

Depending on the connected hardware and configured sensors, LifeShield can work with several types of patient information.

Heart Rate

Monitor heart-rate information received from the connected monitoring device.

SpO₂

Oxygen saturation information can be displayed when supported by the hardware.

Temperature

Temperature information can be received from compatible sensors.

Location

GPS information can help determine the patient's current location when available.

Emergency Response

LifeShield can process configured emergency conditions and initiate the appropriate response workflow.

Fall Detection

A configured fall event can be processed as an emergency condition.

SOS

A patient can initiate an emergency event using the available SOS functionality.

Health Alerts

Configured abnormal health readings can trigger the emergency workflow.

Location Assistance

Available GPS coordinates can be included with emergency information.

Hospital Assistance

LifeShield maintains a registered hospital database containing hospital names, phone numbers, addresses and geographical coordinates.

When patient GPS coordinates are available, the system can calculate distances to the registered hospitals and identify the nearest available hospital in the database.
